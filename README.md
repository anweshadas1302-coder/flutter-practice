### 01 / THE COLLECTION

Not another collection of copied snippets or disconnected experiments.

**The Flutter Archive** is my growing collection of mobile applications, creative implementations, and development experiments — all brought together under one roof.

Every project starts with an idea. Some explore a particular concept, some challenge me to figure out a better implementation, and others are built simply because I wanted to see what I could create.

The common thread? Building, experimenting, and getting better with every application.

### 03 / UNDER THE HOOD

A look at the tools powering these builds.

- **Flutter** — The framework behind the applications.
- **Dart** — The language bringing the logic together.
- **VS Code** — Where the development happens.
- **Git & GitHub** — Where every change finds its place.

### 04 / THE BUILD PHILOSOPHY

I believe the best way to understand a technology is to build with it.

Not every project needs to be groundbreaking. Sometimes a small application teaches an important concept; sometimes an unexpected bug leads to a much better understanding of how things work.

That's what this repository is about — documenting the process, not just showcasing the final product.

### 05 / STILL IN DEVELOPMENT...

This archive isn't meant to stay static.

New applications, better implementations, fresh ideas, and deeper explorations of Flutter will continue to make their way here.

If you're exploring Flutter too, feel free to browse around, learn from the implementations, or share suggestions.
